Our project

Career preparation, brought together.

CareerPilot AI is a group project created to give students an integrated platform for career preparation and guidance. It connects everyday career tasks in one place, from the first resume draft to interview readiness.

The platform combines ATS Resume Analysis, AI Interview Practice, Career Roadmap Generation, Placement Prediction, and an AI Career Chatbot.

What we aim to do

Practical support at every step.

  • Analyse resumes with ATS-focused feedback and skill insights.
  • Help students identify relevant roles and learning goals.
  • Build confidence through AI-assisted interview practice.
  • Create personalised roadmaps and career guidance.
