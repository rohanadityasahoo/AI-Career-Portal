About CareerPilot AI

CareerPilot AI is a group project developed to provide students with an integrated platform for career preparation and guidance.

The platform combines multiple career-focused modules, including ATS Resume Analysis, AI Interview Practice, Job Recommendation, Career Roadmap Generation, Placement Prediction and an AI Career Chatbot.

Project Objectives

  • Analyze resumes using ATS-based techniques.
  • Identify relevant skills from resumes.
  • Recommend suitable career opportunities.
  • Provide AI-assisted interview practice.
  • Generate career learning roadmaps.
  • Provide personalized career guidance through an AI chatbot.
