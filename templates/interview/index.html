Practice role-based interview questions and receive clear, explainable feedback on your answers.

How interview scoring works

This college project uses a curated question bank, organised by job role and difficulty. Each practice interview contains five different questions.

  • The selected role determines the interview domain and question set.
  • Difficulty changes the balance of technical and HR questions.
  • Answers are scored from 0 to 100 using answer detail, explanation, examples, structure, and relevance to the displayed question.
  • The result is guidance for practice, not an official hiring decision.
